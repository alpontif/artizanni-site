Built-Environment Personnel

Engineers, supervisors and skilled artisans supplied and managed for your project.

Projects stall when the right people are not on site. We supply and manage qualified engineers, site supervisors, foremen and artisans for clients' projects, on short or long engagements.

Professionals who want to be considered for placements can register their interest below.

What this covers

  • Site engineers and supervisors
  • Foremen and trade gangs: masons, steel fixers, carpenters, tilers, plumbers, electricians
  • Quantity surveying and planning support
  • HSE officers and storekeepers
  • Vetting, onboarding and site induction
  • Supervision and replacement cover
