Service

Civil, Structural & MEP Engineering

Structural design, civil works, drainage, foundations and building services.

Engineering is our founding discipline. The practice is led by a COREN-registered civil and structural engineer, and our designs come from people who also stand on site when the concrete is poured.

Typical recorded work: an 80-tonne, 60MVA transformer plinth at a 132/33kV substation, substation control building structural design, and the full structural and civil design of a student village (buildings, roads and drainage networks).

What this covers

  • Structural design of reinforced concrete and steel buildings
  • Foundations, retaining structures and heavy equipment plinths
  • Roads, drainage, erosion control and site infrastructure
  • Geotechnical / soil investigation coordination
  • Mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) design and coordination, including solar and back-up power
  • Structural integrity assessments of existing buildings
  • Independent technical review of designs prepared by others
