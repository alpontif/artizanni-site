Artizanni Interiors

Interior design, fit-out, joinery and furnishing in Abuja, Lagos and Port Harcourt.

Artizanni Interiors carries our finishing work: homes, offices, retail and hospitality spaces. Tolerances are visible here, so we plan fit-outs around drying times, delivery dates and the day you need to open.

Fit-outs are run as projects: a fixed programme, a snag list and a handover pack, not an open-ended job.

What this covers

  • Interior design concepts, mood boards and space planning
  • Materials, finishes and FF&E schedules (local and imported)
  • Ceilings, partitions, tiling, stone and decoration
  • Kitchens, wardrobes, counters and bespoke joinery
  • Lighting schemes and electrical fit-out
  • Retail fit-out: shelving, gondolas and display systems
