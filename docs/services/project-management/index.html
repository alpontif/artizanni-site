Service

Project & Property Management

Cost, programme and quality control for your project, then care of the asset.

If you already have a designer or a contractor, we can stand on your side of the table: plan the work, check the quantities, certify valuations and report progress, then manage the property after handover.

Recorded work includes a construction programme, a procurement plan and a ten-year inflation-tied facility and estate management strategy for a student hostel scheme.

What this covers

  • Project planning, programmes and procurement plans
  • Bills of quantities, cost plans and valuation checks
  • Site supervision, quality inspections and progress reports with photos
  • Contract administration, variations and payment certificates
  • Property and facility management: tenant sourcing and screening, lease administration, rent collection
  • Planned and reactive maintenance with documented work orders and service-charge reporting
  • Long-term estate management strategy
