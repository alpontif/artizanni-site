About Artizanni

An engineering practice that builds.

Artizanni Business Services Limited was incorporated in Abuja on 17 September 2012. We design, engineer and build, and we stay accountable for all three.

We started as engineers: substation foundations, dam rehabilitation, drainage and structural design. That discipline now runs through everything we do, from duplexes and retail buildings to fit-outs, land acquisition and our escrow-backed service for Nigerians abroad.

Our recorded work includes design consultancy and specialist sub-contracting for power transmission, water resources and real-estate clients, and design-and-build delivery of homes, commercial and industrial buildings. Artizanni Interiors carries our finishing and fit-out work in Abuja, Lagos and Port Harcourt.

We believe the cheapest building is the one built once. So we put engineers in charge, measure before we price, inspect before we pour and document as we go.

How we work

Principles we hold ourselves to

Sealed by registered professionals

Engineering designs are sealed by COREN-registered engineers and architectural permit drawings by ARCON-registered architects. Nothing is issued for construction without it.

Prices that state their basis

Every estimate shows its date, its accuracy and VAT separately. A firm price follows design and a bill of quantities.

Safety stops work

Anyone on our sites can stop an unsafe activity: unshored trenches, failing props, unprotected work at height or live electrics.

Clients' money is protected

For diaspora clients, funds sit in escrow with First Trustees and are released only against evidence.

Related parties disclosed

Where a group company (plant, materials) supplies your project, we tell you and compare at least one independent quote.

Your information stays private

We collect only what we need, follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and never publish diaspora or secure-space clients.
