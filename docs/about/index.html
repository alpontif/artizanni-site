A contractor that designs.

Artizanni Business Services Limited was incorporated in Abuja on 17 September 2012. We design, engineer and build, and we stay accountable for all three.

Artizanni was incorporated in Abuja in 2012 as a general contractor. What we became was set less by strategy than by the work that came in: earthworks for a transmission substation, then a transformer plinth, then a cofferdam in a live river, then housing, then retail, then the drainage and reclamation schemes that now occupy much of our plant.

Each of those moves taught us something we could not subcontract: how a barrage behaves when you dewater it, how a 60MVA transformer arrives and what it will not forgive, how a retail programme collapses if the ductwork is drawn rather than measured. We kept that knowledge in-house. That is why we design, quantify, programme and build, rather than doing only one of them.

Today the practice works across three connected markets: infrastructure and building construction across Nigeria; property development and project management for Nigerians abroad, structured so clients carry no upfront capital risk; and premium interiors through Artizanni Interiors. One discipline binds them: engineering rigour at every stage, and what is agreed on paper is what is delivered on site.

Our mission

How we work

Principles we hold ourselves to

Sealed by registered professionals

Engineering designs are sealed by COREN-registered engineers and architectural permit drawings by ARCON-registered architects. Nothing is issued for construction without it.

No upfront payments

There are no upfront payments. Every payment is pro rata against certified progress: the client pays Artizanni, and Artizanni pays the team.

Safety stops work

Anyone on our sites can stop an unsafe activity: unshored trenches, failing props, unprotected work at height or live electrics.

Clients' money is protected

For diaspora clients, funds sit in escrow with First Trustees and are released only against evidence.

Related parties disclosed

Where a group company (plant, materials) supplies your project, we tell you and compare at least one independent quote.

Confidential by default

Every brief is under NDA. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and never publish diaspora or secure-space clients.
