Structures in Frame

Reinforced concrete from foundation to roof.

Most of what a builder is judged on is hidden by handover: foundations, column cages, formwork, slab reinforcement, stairs and the blockwork that follows the frame.

Reinforcement is offered for inspection before every pour, and we have provided foundation setting-out for multi-storey framed buildings as a specialist sub-contractor.
