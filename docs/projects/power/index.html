Power Infrastructure

Civil and structural works for 132/33kV transmission substations.

A 60MVA transformer arrives on a programme fixed months earlier and sets down on a plinth whose tolerances were fixed at design stage. We have delivered earthworks design and construction, transformer and equipment foundations, geotechnical investigation, site layout and control-building structural design for substations in South-Western and North-Central Nigeria.

Our scope has also covered access roads, perimeter fencing, borehole water supply and staff quarters: the full civil envelope around the electrical works.
