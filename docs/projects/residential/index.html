Sector 06

Residential Development

Detached and semi-detached homes and estates, from setting out to handover.

Housing is repetitive work, and repetition is where a builder's systems show. We run residential schemes as production: substructure across several plots, then blockwork, roof, render and finishes moving through in sequence so every trade has a continuous working front.

Our work covers 2- and 3-bedroom semi-detached staff quarters on an infrastructure site and private duplexes with classical detailing, arched openings and moulded entrance bays.
