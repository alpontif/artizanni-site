Water Resources & Marine Works

Cofferdams, barrage works, dam rehabilitation and reservoir expansion, built in the wet.

Our water work runs from temporary works in live watercourses to the rehabilitation of dams in service. Dewatering sequences, rock armour, geomembrane protection and working platforms that must survive a rising river shape how we manage risk on every other project.

As specialist sub-contractor we have worked on a cofferdam for the Ogun State Waterworks barrage at Akomoje, Abeokuta (an AFD-funded project), and on reservoir expansion and rehabilitation of irrigation dams in Osun, Nasarawa, Benue and Cross River States.
